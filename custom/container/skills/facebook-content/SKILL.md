# Facebook & Instagram Reels — Cinematic Caption Writing

Phương pháp viết caption chuyên nghiệp, tự nhiên, tối ưu thuật toán mà không dùng engagement bait hay AI-related tags.

---

## Core Formula

```
[Visual Cue] + [Micro Mood] + [Open Interpretation]
```

Ba phần, mỗi phần 1–2 dòng. Tổng caption: **40–80 ký tự lý tưởng**, tối đa 150 ký tự.

---

## Part 1: Visual Cue (dòng đầu)

Dịch cảm giác của hình ảnh, không mô tả nội dung. 3–8 từ.

| Descriptive ❌ | Cinematic ✅ |
|---|---|
| "Sunset over the ocean" | "Light folding into itself" |
| "Woman walking alone" | "One figure, unmeasured distance" |
| "Mountains in fog" | "The world before it remembered its shape" |
| "Hands holding coffee" | "Warmth located, briefly" |

**Cách đọc một video trước khi viết:**
1. **Color temp**: Warm (amber/gold) hay Cold (blue/silver)?
2. **Pace**: Fast cuts (<2s) hay long take?
3. **Framing**: Tight (close-up) hay Wide (landscape)?
4. **Light**: Hard/directional hay Soft/diffused?
5. **Camera**: Static / Handheld / Tracking / Aerial?

**Matching vocabulary:**

| Video signature | Vocabulary register |
|---|---|
| Warm + slow + wide | longing, expansive, long phrases |
| Cold + fast + tight | clipped lines, active verbs, present tense |
| Warm + static + tight | stillness, private, short intimate lines |
| Cold + slow + wide | sparse, solitude, near-silent phrasing |

---

## Part 2: Micro Mood (giữa caption)

Đặt tên cảm xúc ở mức **species**, không phải genus. Một cụm danh từ, không giải thích.

| Broad ❌ | Micro Mood ✅ |
|---|---|
| Lonely | "The quiet after a decision" |
| Nostalgic | "Memory arriving without permission" |
| Peaceful | "The moment before you have to become someone again" |
| In love | "Noticing something twice" |
| Lost | "Moving without a particular destination" |

**Micro Mood Library** (dùng hoặc dùng làm mẫu):

*Impermanence:*
- "Knowing it will not last, staying anyway"
- "Everything that ends without announcement"

*Solitude:*
- "Alone in the right way"
- "The hour no one else was part of"

*Transition:*
- "Between the version you left and the version you are becoming"
- "Moving away from something without moving toward anything yet"

*Longing:*
- "Called to somewhere you cannot name"
- "Missing a place you were not always comfortable in"

*Arrival:*
- "Warmth located, briefly"
- "Something waiting that did not know it was waiting"

*Departure:*
- "Leaving before it became too much to leave"
- "Going somewhere was enough reason to leave"

## Part 3: Open Interpretation (dòng cuối)

Không hỏi. Không CTA tường minh. Kết thúc ở ngưỡng cửa, không phải kết luận.

**Tại sao hiệu quả:** Zeigarnik Effect — câu chưa hoàn chỉnh tạo cognitive tension, người đọc cần "xả" bằng cách comment. Họ comment vì muốn, không vì được yêu cầu.

**Kỹ thuật:**
1. **Universal "you"**: "You have been here." / "You kept that one."
2. **Named absence**: "For whoever is still finding the way back."
3. **Two truths unresolved**: "It was leaving. It was also arriving."
4. **Threshold, not outcome**: "The step before the answer."
5. **Sentence that just stops**: "Still learning how to ___"

**Passive CTA vs Direct CTA:**

| Direct CTA ❌ | Passive (Open Interpretation) ✅ |
|---|---|
| "Can you relate? Comment below." | "Or maybe it was already yours." |
| "Tag someone who needs this." | "For whoever needed the long way around." |
| "Where do you want to travel?" | "Some places stay. Some places you stay in." |

## 12 Caption Examples

**01 — Slow tracking, empty market, warm light**
```
Warm light, unoccupied.

The morning that belongs to no one in particular.

Some hours are just yours to move through.
```

**02 — Cold drone, mountains at dawn, foggy**
```
Before the world had arranged itself.

The kind of stillness that doesn't wait for you.

You arrived anyway.
```

**03 — Tight handheld, hands making coffee, warm interior**
```
One warm thing.

Warmth located, briefly.

This is enough.
```

**04 — Fast-cut city at night, neon, cold**
```
Every stranger. Mid-chapter.
The city keeps rewriting itself.

You are somewhere in the draft.
```

**05 — Wide landscape, single figure walking away, golden**
```
The road kept its direction.
The figure kept its uncertainty.

Going somewhere
was enough reason to leave.
```

**06 — Close-up rain on window, blurred warm light outside**
```
The other side of the glass.

All that warm light, still available.

You were deciding whether to go toward it.
```

**07 — Slow-mo autumn leaves falling, golden, soft focus**
```
Everything amber.
Everything ending well.

Impermanence in a good light.
```

**08 — Empty room after gathering, afternoon light through blinds**
```
After the people,
the room still holds their warmth.

Something was decided here.
You were not there for it.
```

**09 — Aerial cold ocean, single small boat**
```
Ocean with no particular interest in the boat.

The boat, going anyway.

Some crossings are only about crossing.
```

**10 — Close-up face listening in conversation, warm interior**
```
The face before the words arrive.

Whatever was said
had already been received.

You know this expression.
```

**11 — Handheld, following someone up dark staircase, warm light at top**
```
The top of the stairs was already warm.
The climb was still necessary.

You have been most alive
in transit.
```

**12 — Wide, cold dawn, empty station, train arriving in fog**
```
Cold platform.
Light arriving from far away.

The one who waits
has already made the harder choice.
```

## Caption Rhythm Rules

Caption rhythm = video edit pace.

- **Fast video** → Short lines. Hard stops. Active verbs. Present tense.
- **Slow video** → Long phrases. Soft line breaks. Open syntax.

**Punctuation as breath:**
- `.` — Full stop. Thought lands, does not continue.
- `—` — Thought still moving, but you've left: "something in the light—"
- No punctuation at line end — enjambment, forward momentum.
- `...` — Trailing. Use sparingly or it weakens the line.

**Test:** Read caption aloud at the pace of the video. If it feels wrong, rewrite.

## Neutral Global English

Tránh idiom, slang vùng miền, từ văn hóa specific.

| Avoid ❌ | Use ✅ |
|---|---|
| "Hitting different" | "Something is shifted" |
| "Vibes" | The actual tone: stillness / weight / warmth |
| "It's giving ___" | Describe directly |
| "Lowkey" | Remove entirely |
| "Bloody gorgeous" | "Extraordinary light" |
| Sports/weather metaphors | Remove |

**Vocabulary that travels globally:**
- Sensory: light, dark, weight, warmth, cold, distance, silence
- Temporal: before, after, still, already, not yet, the space between
- Spatial: close, far, inside, outside, the edge of, opening
- Emotional (descriptive, not labelled): "the kind of quiet that arrives after" not "peaceful"

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Visual analysis done (color temp / pace / framing / light / camera)
- [ ] Visual Cue: 3–8 words, sensation not description, no evaluative adjectives
- [ ] Micro Mood: species-level precision, noun phrase, no explanation
- [ ] Open Interpretation: no direct question, ends at threshold, universal "you" or named absence
- [ ] Caption read aloud — rhythm matches video pace
- [ ] No idioms, no slang, no regional references
- [ ] 3–5 hashtags, no AI tags, no spam tags
- [ ] At least 1 line someone might screenshot/save
- [ ] Caption works with sound off
